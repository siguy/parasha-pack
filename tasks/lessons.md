# Lessons (corrections from Simon)

## 2026-10-05 — Don't drop an option when a new default is chosen
- **What happened:** Simon said "maximize 8.5×11 for home printing now," and I wrote that 5×7, bleed and CMYK were "gone."
- **Rule:** When Simon picks a new default, keep the old capability as an optional or secondary path unless he explicitly says to delete it. Ask "replace or add?" if it's unclear. "For now" signals that the old path should stay available.

## 2026-10-05 — Don't pick a format Simon didn't ask for
- **What happened:** I specified the teacher booklet as half-letter and saddle-stitched without asking. Simon wants 8.5×11.
- **Rule:** Default every printable (cards, booklet, extras) to the format Simon already chose (8.5×11 letter) unless he asks for a different one. List any new physical format as a question, not as a decision.

## 2026-10-06 — Stick to the Torah text; keep it simple
- **What happened:** my Day 1 art brief included water (from 1:2), and the backs said "tov" on every day. Both are confusing or inaccurate: tov appears 7 times, none on Day 2 and twice on Day 3.
- **Rule:** every card's wording and image comes only from that card's verses (the Torah Scholar's `in_text` list). Quote the Hebrew from `research/{parasha}.yaml`, never from memory. One clear focal subject. Midrash goes only in the guide, labeled, with a source.
