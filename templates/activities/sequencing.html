How to play

Mix up one set of {{ n_cards }} cards. The child lays them in a row, left to right, and tells the story: “First…, then…, next…, last…”. Then turn over the control strip and check together. Two sets let two children (or two teams) play side by side.

Age 4 Three cards

  • Use only {{ easy|join(", ") }}. Say “first, next, last”.
  • Lay the control strip face up at first, so it is a matching game.

Age 5 All {{ n_cards }} cards

  • Control strip face down. Child orders all {{ n_cards }}, then checks.
  • Retell each card with its movement from the story (tree arms, swim hands, rest…).

  • Lay out all {{ n_cards }} in order. Children close their eyes; take one away. Which card is missing?
