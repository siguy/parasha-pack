Card Back v3: mockups

All 7 back types for Purim, drawn at ⅓ of print size (the real card is 5×7 in, 1500×2100 px). Each one is written to fit the new word limits: a goal of 10 words or fewer, SAY in 50 words or fewer, and at most 2 questions. The teacher reads it top to bottom. Background, extra activities, and "what happened to Haman?" are in the printed teacher guide.

Bold = what the teacher reads aloud ▸ Colored chip = what the teacher/children do ? = question · ✋ = children can answer without words ★ CORE = do this card if you're short on time

Design notes

AnchorSpotlight StoryConnection TraditionPower Word Home
  • Colors: every color is readable with white text and on the cream background (contrast of at least 4.5:1, the accessibility minimum). The current gold is 2.2:1. Tradition has its own teal now instead of sharing Spotlight's gold. Each card type also has its own icon, so color-blind teachers can tell Story (red) from Power Word (green).
  • Same look as today: same cream background, colored header bar, Assistant font for English and Heebo for Hebrew, and the colored stripe on the left of the script. It's an update of the current back, not a new style.
  • Nothing gets cut off: the export will stop with an error if any text doesn't fit, so a script can't be silently cut mid-sentence again.
  • Draft Hebrew: the Hebrew on this page (especially the home card's Shabbat question) still needs your review.
  • Not shown yet: fronts, print bleed, and the guide booklet. Those are later phases.
