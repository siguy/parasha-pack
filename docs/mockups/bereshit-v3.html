Bereshit: v3 card mockups (letter size)

This is the whole Bereshit deck (10 cards) printed on 8.5×11. The white edge is the 0.3" page margin, so a home printer can print the full card and nobody has to cut anything. Each card is one sheet: art on the front, the teacher back on the reverse. Art below is a placeholder; real art comes in Phase 6.

Bold = the teacher reads it aloud ▸ Chip = the teacher or children do it ? question · ✋ children can answer without words ★ CORE = do this card if time is short

1 · Fronts (what the children see)

The title sits in the calm top band of the art. A type chip at bottom-left tells the teacher the card type without relying on color. Story cards have a number at top-right so the deck works as a sequencing game, and a Hebrew word badge at bottom-right.

2 · All 10 backs (what the teacher reads)

At letter size the type is about 30% larger than on 5×7, so a teacher holding the card up can still read it. Every back keeps to the v3 limits: goal ≤10 words, SAY ≤50 words, at most 2 questions.

3 · The same design at 5×7 (optional vendor print)

The same component exported in 5×7 mode. The red dashed line is the trim line; past it is the 0.125" bleed for a print shop. Nothing is redesigned: sizes scale with the card.

Notes & open items

Hebrew for you to check: the home-card question ״מָה הַדָּבָר הָאָהוּב עֲלֵיכֶם שֶׁה׳ בָּרָא?״, the Chava gesture word עוֹזְרִים, and the connection title שׁוֹמְרִים. I wrote plural forms on purpose so they work for any group of children.
  • Guide page numbers follow the planned letter-size booklet layout (p.2 week plan, p.4–5 spotlights, p.6–9 stories, p.10 connection, p.11 power word). The validator will check every back's "Guide p.N" against that layout.
  • Week plan: Shabbat (Story 3) is taught on Friday, right before Shabbat. Story 4 (naming the animals) comes earlier, on Day 3, alongside Chava.
  • Snake and fruit: not on any card. Guide p.9 gives the teacher a calm, scripted answer if a child asks.
  • Placeholder art: the fronts use simple drawings only to show where the title zone and crop lines fall. Real art comes from Nano Banana 2 in Phase 6.
