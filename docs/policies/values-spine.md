# Values Spine (DRAFT — Simon to edit)

Each deck teaches exactly ONE middah. The list repeats through the year so children
meet each value 3–5 times (spiral). The Series Planner (agent 00) assigns them in series.yaml.

| # | Middah | Hebrew | Kid phrase | Example decks |
|---|--------|--------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | Kindness | חֶסֶד | "Doing something nice without being asked" | Vayera (Avraham's guests), Chayei Sarah (Rivka) |
| 2 | Courage | אֹמֶץ לֵב | "Being brave even when you feel scared" | Purim, Beshalach (Nachshon), Shemot |
| 3 | Trust in Hashem | אֱמוּנָה | "Knowing Hashem is always with us" | Lech Lecha, Beshalach |
| 4 | Gratitude | הַכָּרַת הַטּוֹב | "Saying thank you — and noticing good things" | Vayetzei (Leah naming Yehuda), Sukkot, Ki Tavo |
| 5 | Humility | עֲנָוָה | "Not needing to be the biggest" | Beha'alotecha (Moshe), Purim (contrast w/ Haman) |

Rules:
- One middah per deck; the anchor objective and the home card both name it.
- No middah may repeat within 4 consecutive decks.
- Each middah gets ONE fixed hand gesture used every time it appears (builds ritual).
